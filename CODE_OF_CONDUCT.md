# Skedra Community Code of Conduct

## Our community

Skedra welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Everyone participating in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and official
community spaces is expected to help keep them respectful and welcoming.
These expectations also apply when representing the project in public.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful of different perspectives.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code, rather than attacking people.
- Accept feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and help others learn.
- Respect people's privacy and their boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, threats, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are
not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer at
[support@skedra.xyz](mailto:support@skedra.xyz), with the subject
"Skedra community conduct". Include relevant links, context, and any immediate
concerns. Avoid posting private reports or personal information in public issues.

Reports will be reviewed by the maintainer. Information will be shared only as
needed to investigate and respond. Retaliation against someone who reports a
concern or participates in a review is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates this code, explain the
expected change, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation, depending on the severity and pattern of behavior. The same
standards apply to maintainers and contributors.

If you believe a moderation decision was mistaken, contact the address above
privately with the relevant context for reconsideration.
